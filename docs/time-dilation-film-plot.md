## Scene 1 — The same four years (8 seconds)

Anu: "Four years! They flew by."
Dev: "Really? Mine felt endless."
Narrator: "Same years. Different feelings."

## Scene 2 — Feeling versus measuring (8 seconds)
Use two restrained cinematic panels: the students sharing a quiet conversation, then a student waiting alone in an examination hall.
