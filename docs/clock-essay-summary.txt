Hi y'all. A clock can tell you how much time has passed, but not what that time meant. An hour of waiting can feel endless; an afternoon with someone you love can disappear. Physics has its own remarkable story: motion and gravity can make clocks record different amounts of time. Our memories are a different kind of story, not a proof of relativity. The same four years of college can leave each of us with a different lifetime of moments. Clocks measure the duration. We give it depth.
